Your operating name is the name you use in your day-to-day activities and to advertise your business. You only need to provide your operating name if it is different from your legal name.

  • Example for sole proprietor

    You are a sole proprietor, your name is John Smith, and you advertise your business as ABC Framing. In this case, your legal business name is John Smith and your operating name is ABC Framing.

    If you decide to change your operating name to ABC Portraits, you must advise the Canada Revenue Agency of the change.

  • Example for corporation

    The corporation's name is 12345 Canada Inc. and the business advertises as ABC Properties. In this case, the legal business name is 12345 Canada Inc. and the operating name is ABC Properties.

    If the operating name changes to ABC Management Properties, that is considered an operating name change and you must notify the Canada Revenue Agency.

Ways to change a business operating name

The fastest and easiest way to change your business operating name is to sign to your CRA account to make this change online.


Online

Processing time: Immediate

You can change or delete an existing business operating or trade name, or add a new one in your CRA account. There is no wait time and it is updated immediately after you submit the changes.

Steps to change a business operating name

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Business to access My Business Account
  3. Select Profile on the Overview page
  4. Select Manage operating names under the Operating names section
  5. Choose one of the following:
    • Delete to remove an existing operating name
    • Edit to correct an existing operating name (maximum of 175 characters)
    • +Add a new operating name to add a new operating name (maximum of 175 characters)
  6. Confirm changes and select Submit

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Enter the 9-digit Business number (BN) of the business you are representing and select Access BN
  4. Go to Step 3 for changing business operating name

To change a business address online for someone else, you must be an authorized representative who can access and update their business tax information.

If you are unable to access your CRA business information

To access your business information online, you must already have your BN connected to your CRA account.

For details: Access your business information online in your CRA account


By phone

Processing time: Same day

Information you will need to verify your identity

  • Business number (BN)
  • Business name
  • Complete business address
  • Assessed return, notice of assessment, reassessment, or other tax document for your business

You can also be signed in to your CRA account to access your business tax information.

Call on behalf of someone else

You may only call on behalf of someone else if:

  • You are the representative of the business

    You are the business' representative who is authorized to call on their behalf.

    For details: Authorize a representative

  • Business owner you are representing is present

    The business owner you are representing must be present on the call to give their verbal consent.

By mail or fax

Processing time: Up to 10 business days after we receive your request

To change your business operating name by mail or fax, you must send a written request to your tax centre.

  1. Include the following information:
    • Your signature
    • Your currrent business operating name
    • Your business number (BN)
    • Your new business operating name
    • Effective date of the change
  2. Send us your request:

    • By mail

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. Mail your request to your tax centre

    • By fax

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. To find the corresponding fax number for your tax centre, refer to Tax centres
      3. Send your request using the fax number for your tax centre

Make a change for someone else

To make this change for someone else, you must be an authorized representative with access to update their business tax information. The written request must clearly indicate you are making the change on behalf of the business and include your signature.
