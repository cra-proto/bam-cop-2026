The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is using online mail delivery as the default method for sending most business correspondence. Instead of receiving paper mail, the CRA will send your business notices and other correspondence through My Business Account in your CRA account. You must sign in to your CRA account to view your online mail.

Types of business correspondence you may receive

You may receive certain business correspondence including:

  • Letters (instalment reminders, endorsement fee letter, PAD modification letter)
  • Notices (notice of assessment, notice of reassessment)
  • Notifications
  • Statements

If you made an enquiry through My Business in your CRA account, you may receive responses from the CRA for these electronic enquiries including:

  • Response to a formal dispute (notice of objection, appeal)
  • Response to an enquiry
  • Response to an audit enquiry submission

The CRA will use your business email address on file to send a notification when you have new mail in your CRA account. If you prefer to receive paper copies of your business correspondences, you can update your business mail preferences. Paper delivery will resume using the existing business address the CRA has on file.

If you are registered for CRA email notifications and choose to receive paper mail, you will receive your business correspondence by paper mail, but may also receive email notifications.

Ways to manage how you receive your business correspondence

The fastest and easiest way to change how you receive your business correspondence is to sign to your CRA account to make this change online.


Online

Processing time: Immediate

You can manage how you receive your business correspondence in your CRA account. It is updated immediately after you submit your changes.

Steps to change a business phone number

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Business to access My Business Account
  3. Select Profile on the Overview page
  4. Select Manage mail for my business under the Manage Mail for my Business section
  5. Choose the delivery method you no longer want to receive (Online or Paper)
  6. Choose the program accounts you want to apply the change to
  7. Confirm your requested choices
  8. Confirm changes and select Submit

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Enter the 9-digit Business number (BN) of the business you are representing and select Access BN
  4. Continue with Step 3 from changing how you receive your business correspondence

To change a business address online for someone else, you must be an authorized representative who can access and update their business tax information.


By phone

Processing time: Same day

Information you will need to verify your identity

  • Business number (BN)
  • Business name
  • Complete business address
  • Assessed return, notice of assessment, reassessment, or other tax document for your business

You can also be signed in to your CRA account to access your business tax information.

Call on behalf of someone else

You may only call on behalf of someone else if:

  • You are the representative of the business

    You are the business' representative who is authorized to call on their behalf.

    For details: Authorize a representative

  • Person you are representing is present during the call

    The person you are representing must be present on the call to give their verbal consent.

By mail or fax

Processing time: x to x weeks after we receive your documents

To mail or fax your business correspondence preferences to the CRA, you must send us a letter.

Follow these steps to change your business correspondence preferences by mail:

  1. Include the following information in your letter:
    • Your signature
    • Your business name
    • Your business number (BN)
    • Your business correspondeance preferences
    • Effective date of the change
    • Which CRA program accounts need to be updated with the new phone number (or indicate if all accounts need to be updated)
  2. Send us your letter:

    • By mail

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. Mail your letter to your tax centre

    • By fax

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. To find the corresponding fax number for your tax centre, refer to Tax centres
      3. Send your letter using the fax number for your tax centre

Make a change for someone else

To change a business address for someone else, you must be an authorized representative with access to update their business tax information. The letter must clearly indicate you are making the change on behalf of the business with your signature.
