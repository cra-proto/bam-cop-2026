To access your business information online, you must link the Social Insurance Number (SIN) of an owner, partner, or director to the business number (BN) of your business. The BN can then be added to your CRA account. If a SIN is not provided to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), you will not be able to access your business information online.

For a corporation, the SIN of a director or officer must be linked to the corporation's BN in order to:

  • Access My Business Account in their CRA account
  • Authorize representatives for online access

If a director's or officer's SIN was provided when the corporation registered for a BN, the information will automatically be updated with the CRA. You must still add the BN to your CRA account to access My Business Account.

If you do not know your BN or are not sure if you already have one, go to Ways to find an existing business number.

Email notifications when a SIN is updated

The CRA will send an email notification to the email address associated with your T1 personal income tax account when your SIN is added or updated on a business account. These notifications help you identify unauthorized activity involving your SIN to better protect your personal and business account information. Make sure your personal email address is up to date with the CRA.

You will receive an email notification if:

  • Your SIN is added as a new owner on a business account
  • Your SIN information is updated on a business account
  • Your SIN is added to an existing owner record on a business account

Ways to add a SIN to your business

If the SIN of an owner, partner, or director has not been provided to the CRA, the fastest way to link your SIN to your business is online through your CRA account.


Online

Processing time: Immediate

You can link the SIN of an owner, director, or officer to your BN online through your CRA account. There is no wait time and it is updated immediately after you submit the changes.

Steps to add a SIN to your CRA account

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select +Add account on the Welcome page
  3. Select Business account
  4. Select Add an existing business number (BN)
  5. Enter your 9-digit Business number and select Add
  6. Choose the situation which applies:

    • SIN already linked to your BN

      Select Submit and your BN will be added to your CRA once the information is validated.

    • SIN not linked to your BN

      Answer Yes or No when asked "Have you filed a GST/HST return or a T2 corporation income tax return?" and select Submit.

      • Yes is your answer

        Enter the requested information from your assessed return

      • No is your answer

        • If your BN was registered within the last 28 days, select Submit
        • If it is more than 28 days, you must get a representative to submit it online or send it by mail or fax
  7. Choose the situation which applies:

    • SIN already linked to your BN

      Select Submit and your BN will be added to your CRA once the information is validated.

    • SIN not linked to your BN

      Answer Yes or No when asked "Have you filed a GST/HST return or a T2 corporation income tax return?" and select Submit.

      If your answer is Yes:

      • Enter the requested information from your assessed return

      If your answer is No, follow the instructions to either:

      • Select Submit if your BN was registered within the last 28 days
      • Get a representative to submit your SIN online or send it by mail or fax if your BN was registered more than 28 days ago
  8. Confirm changes and select Submit

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

Processing time: Within 10 business days (2 weeks)

To submit the request online using the "Submit documents" service, the request must be signed and dated by the director or officer with the SIN to be added to the BN.

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Under the Correspondence menu section, select Submit documents
  4. Select Update to owner/legal representative/executor/trustee under Select client type
  5. Choose No when asked about a case or reference number
  6. Choose Add or modify a business owner/director from Topic dropdown box
  7. Enter the 9-digit Business number and select Next
  8. Select + Attachments to upload the signed request
  9. Browse to add your file, enter a short description for your document, and select Upload file(s)
  10. Confirm changes and select Submit

To make this change on behalf of a business, you must be an authorized representative with permission to access and update the business' tax information.

If you are unable to access your CRA business information

To access your business information online, your BN must be added to your CRA account.

For details: Access your business information online in your CRA account


By mail or fax

Processing time: Up to 30 business days after we receive your request

If you cannot provide your SIN online, you can submit a signed request to have your SIN added to your CRA account by mail or fax.

  1. Include the following:
    • Signature of the requesting director or officer
    • Director's or officer's SIN to be added
    • Business name
    • Business number (BN)
    • Effective date of the change
  2. Send us your signed request:

    • By mail

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. Mail your request to your tax centre

    • By fax

      1. Find your tax centre using Find a CRA address
      2. Send your request to your tax centre using the corresponding fax number:
        • Prince Edward Island Tax Centre
          1-833-724-7237
        • Sudbury Tax Centre
          1-855-276-1529

Make a change on behalf of a business

To make this change on behalf of a business, you must be an authorized representative with permission to access and update the business's tax information. The written request must clearly state you are making the change on behalf of the business and include your signature (and RAC identifier if applicable).
