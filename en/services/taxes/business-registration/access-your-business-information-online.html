You may be looking for: Register for a CRA account

To access your business information online, you need to link your Social Insurance Number (SIN) to your business number (BN) and add your BN to your CRA account.

Once set up, you can view and manage your CRA program accounts, and authorize representatives using My Business Account in your CRA account.

Only owners, partners, or directors whose name and SIN are on file with the CRA have the authority to:

  • Access the business information
  • Make changes to the business
  • Add representatives
  • Access My Business Account

Steps to add a BN to your CRA account

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select + Add account
  3. Select Business account
  4. Select Add an existing business number (BN)
  5. Enter your 9-digit Business number and select Add
  6. Choose the business number from your profile and select Add a business

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Enter the 9-digit Business number (BN) of the business you are representing and select Access BN
  4. Go to Step 3 for changing a business address

To change a business address online for someone else, you must be an authorized representative who can access and update their business tax information.

If you are unable to access your CRA business information

To access your business information online, you must already have your BN connected to your CRA account. For details: Access your business information online in your CRA account
