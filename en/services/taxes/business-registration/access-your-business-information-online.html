To access your business information online with the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), your Social Insurance Number (SIN) must be linked to your business number (BN). Once linked, you must also add your BN to your CRA account.

Only owners, partners, or directors whose name and SIN are on file with the CRA will have access to:

  • View and update business information
  • Make changes to the business
  • Manage authorized representatives

What you need for online access

If you are accessing your business information online for the first time:

  • You must have a CRA account
  • Your SIN must be linked to your BN
  • Your BN must be added to your CRA account

Steps to access your business information online

  1. Confirm you have a CRA account

    You must have a CRA account to be able to access your business tax information online. You must register for a CRA account if you do not have one.

  2. Verify your SIN has been linked to your BN

    Before the business's tax information can be accessed online, the CRA must have the SIN on file of the owner, partners, or directors who need access to your business information online.

    The SIN is already linked to your BN when:

    • The BN was registered with the CRA
    • The owner, partner, or director was added to the business

    If the SIN is not linked to your BN, you may be able to link it when you add the BN to the CRA account.

  3. Add your BN to your CRA account

    Intro


    Online

    Processing time: Immediate

    You can link the SIN of an owner, director, or officer to your BN online through your CRA account. It is updated immediately after you submit the changes.

    Steps to add a SIN and BN to your CRA account

    1. Sign in to your CRA account
    2. Select +Add account on the Welcome page
    3. Select Business account
    4. Select Add an existing business number (BN)
    5. Enter your 9-digit Business number and select Add

    6. Choose the situation which applies:

      • SIN already linked to your BN

        Select Submit and your BN will be added to your CRA once the information is validated.

      • SIN not linked to your BN

        Choose an answer based on if you've filed a GST/HST return or T2 corporation income tax return:

        • If Yes, select Submit and enter the requested information from your assessed return
        • If No and you registered your BN within the last 28 days, select Submit
        • If No and you registered your BN more than 28 days ago, you cannot add your SIN online and must use one of the other options
    7. Confirm changes and select Submit

    CRA sign in

    For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

    Processing time: Within 10 business days (2 weeks)

    To submit the request online using the "Submit documents" service, the request must be signed and dated by the director or officer with the SIN to be added to the BN.

    If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

    1. Sign in to your CRA account
    2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
    3. Under the Correspondence menu section, select Submit documents
    4. Select Update to owner/legal representative/executor/trustee under Select client type
    5. Choose No when asked about a case or reference number
    6. Choose Add or modify a business owner/director from Topic dropdown box
    7. Enter the 9-digit Business number and select Next
    8. Select + Attachments to upload the signed request
    9. Browse to add your file, enter a short description for your document, and select Upload file(s)
    10. Confirm changes and select Submit

    To make this change on behalf of a business, you must be an authorized representative with permission to access and update the business's tax information.


    By phone

    Processing time: Same day

    Information you will need to verify your identity

    • Business number (BN)
    • Business name
    • Complete business address
    • Assessed return, notice of assessment, reassessment, or other tax document for your business

    You can also be signed in to your CRA account to access your business tax information.

    Call on behalf of someone else

    You may only call on behalf of someone else if:

    • You are the representative of the business

      You are the business' representative who is authorized to call on their behalf.

      For details: Authorize a representative

    • Business owner you are representing is present

      The business owner you are representing must be present on the call to give their verbal consent.

    By mail or fax

    Processing time: Up to 30 business days after we receive your request

    If you cannot provide your SIN online, you can submit a signed request to have your SIN added to your CRA account by mail or fax.

    1. Include the following:
      • Signature of the requesting director or officer
      • Director's or officer's SIN to be added
      • Business name
      • Business number (BN)
      • Effective date of the change
    2. Send us your signed request:

      • By mail

        1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
        2. Mail your request to your tax centre

      • By fax

        1. Find your tax centre using Find a CRA address
        2. Send your request to your tax centre using the corresponding fax number:
          • Prince Edward Island Tax Centre
            1-833-724-7237
          • Sudbury Tax Centre
            1-855-276-1529

    Make a change on behalf of a business

    To make this change on behalf of a business, you must be an authorized representative with permission to access and update the business's tax information. The written request must clearly state you are making the change on behalf of the business and include your signature (and RAC identifier if applicable).
