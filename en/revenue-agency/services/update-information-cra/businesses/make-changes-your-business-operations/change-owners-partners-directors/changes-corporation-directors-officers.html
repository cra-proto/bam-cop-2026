The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) uses the director and officer information on file to confirm who can act on behalf of a corporation. When a director or officer joins or leaves or if their information changes, you need to update the corporation's records with the CRA as soon as possible. This ensures your directors and officers have the appropriate permissions to access and manage the corporation's tax information.

If your information is not up to date, it can delay access to managing corporate tax information or representative authorizations in My Business Account in your CRA account.

Who can make a change

To access or update a corporation's tax information, you must be one of the following:

  • A director or officer
  • An authorized representative already listed on the account with Level 2 or 3 access
  • A legal representative
