To access your business information online, you must link the Social Insurance Number (SIN) of an owner, partner, or director to the business number (BN) of your business. The BN can then be added to your CRA account. If a SIN is not provided to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), you will not be able to access your business information online.

A director's or officer's SIN must be linked to the BN to:

  • Access My Business Account
  • Authorize representatives for online access

If the SIN was already provided during BN registration, the CRA will update its records. However, you must still add the BN to your CRA account to access My Business Account.

If you do not know your BN or are not sure if you already have one, go to Ways to find an existing business number.
