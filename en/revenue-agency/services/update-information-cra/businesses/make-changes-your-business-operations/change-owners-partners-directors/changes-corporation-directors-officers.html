The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) uses the director and officer information on file to confirm who can act on behalf of a corporation. When a director or officer joins or leaves or if their information changes, you need to update the corporation's records with the CRA as soon as possible. This ensures your directors and officers have the appropriate permissions to access and manage the corporation's tax information.

If your information is not up to date, it can delay access to managing corporate tax information or representative authorizations in My Business Account in your CRA account.

Who can make a change

To access or update a corporation's tax information, you must be one of the following:

  • A director or officer
  • An authorized representative already listed on the account with Level 2 or 3 access
  • A legal representative

Update director or officer information

The following steps are for updating Canadian directors or officers of a corporation.

If you need to update the residency status of non-resident directors or officers of either a Canadian or non-resident corporation, refer to Update residency status of a non-resident director or officer.

  1. Update the corporate registry 

    You must update changes to director or officer information with the federal or your provincial incorporating authority which registered your corporation. How long it takes for the update to show up on the corporate registry depends on the incorporating authority and must be confirmed before continuing with the next step.

    It may take 24 to 48 hours for the CRA to receive updates from corporate registries.

  2. Determine if you need to notify the CRA directly

    You must contact the CRA directly for any of the following:

    • Updates to officer information
    • Changes to a director's or officer's SIN or residency status
    • Corporation is registered outside of Canada or in certain provinces or territories

    Find out which corporations need to notify the CRA directly based on where their corporation was registered:

    • Need to send updates directly to the CRA

      You must send your updates directly to the CRA if your corporation is registered in one of the following:

      • Quebec
      • Newfoundland and Labrador
      • Northwest Territories
      • Yukon
      • Nunavut
      • Corporate registry outside of Canada

    • Automatically shared with the CRA

      No action is needed, but it may take 24 to 48 hours after you've updated the corporate registries before the CRA receives them.

      Your updates will be shared automatically with the CRA if your corporation is registered in one of the following:

      • British Columbia
      • Alberta
      • Saskatchewan
      • Manitoba
      • Ontario
      • New Brunswick
      • Prince Edward Island
      • Nova Scotia
