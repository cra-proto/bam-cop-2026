When you provide a business email address to the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA), you are automatically registering to receive email notifications from the CRA. These notifications let you know when you have new mail to view in your CRA account or when important changes were made on your account. If you change your business email address, you must notify the CRA as soon as possible to ensure you do not miss any important mail.

If there was mail ready to send to your before you added an email address, you may still receive correspondence by paper mail instead of receiving it in your CRA account.

Ways to change your business email address

The fastest and easiest way to change your business email address is to sign to your CRA account to make this change online.


Online

Processing time: Immediate

You can add, change, or delete a business email address in your CRA account. It is updated immediately after you submit your changes.

Steps to change a business email address

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Business to access My Business Account
  3. Select Profile on the Overview page
  4. Select Manage notification preferences under the Notification Preferences section
  5. Choose one of the following:
    • Select Register email address and enter a new email address
    • Select Edit, choose the email address to edit, and enter the new email address
    • Select Delete, choose the email address to remove and accounts it applies to
  6. Confirm changes and select Submit

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 2 or 3 authorization

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Enter the 9-digit Business number (BN) of the business you are representing and select Access BN
  4. Go to Step 3 for changing a business email address

To change a business address online for someone else, you must be an authorized representative who can access and update their business tax information.

If you are unable to access your CRA business information

To access your business information online, you must already have your BN connected to your CRA account.

For details: Access your business information online in your CRA account


By phone

Processing time: Same day

Information you will need to verify your identity

  • Business number (BN)
  • Business name
  • Complete business address
  • Assessed return, notice of assessment, reassessment, or other tax document for your business

You can also be signed in to your CRA account to access your business tax information.

Call on behalf of someone else

You may only call on behalf of someone else if:

  • You are the representative of the business

    You are the business' representative who is authorized to call on their behalf.

    For details: Authorize a representative

  • Person you are representing is present during the call

    The person you are representing must be present on the call to give their verbal consent.

By mail or fax

Processing time: x to x weeks after we receive your documents

To mail or fax your business address change to the CRA, you must send us a letter.

Follow these steps to change your address by mail:

  1. Include the following information in your letter:
    • Your signature
    • Your business name
    • Your business number (BN)
    • Your old business email address
    • Your new business email address
    • Effective date of the change
    • Which CRA program accounts need to be updated with the new email address (or indicate if all accounts need to be updated)

    If you are changing more than one eamil address related to your business, you must list all of the eamil addresses on file you want to change.

  2. Send us your letter:

    • By mail

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. Mail your letter to your tax centre

    • By fax

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. To find the corresponding fax number for your tax centre, refer to Tax centres
      3. Send your letter using the fax number for your tax centre

Make a change for someone else

To change a business address for someone else, you must be an authorized representative with access to update their business tax information. The letter must clearly indicate you are making the change on behalf of the business with your signature.
