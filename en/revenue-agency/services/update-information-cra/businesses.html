Inform the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) about changes to your business profile information or if you are making changes to your business operations.

Services and information

Update your business profile information

Change owners, partners, or directors, business address, operating name, phone number, email or mail options

Make changes to your business operations

Close program accounts, stop or resume operations, change legal status, fiscal year-end, buy or sell a business
