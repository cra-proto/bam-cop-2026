The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is using online mail delivery as the default method for sending most business correspondence. Instead of receiving paper mail, the CRA will send your business notices and other correspondence through My Business Account in your CRA account. You must sign in to your CRA account to view your online mail.

Types of business correspondence you may receive

You may receive certain business correspondence including:

  • Letters (instalment reminders, endorsement fee letter, PAD modification letter)
  • Notices (notice of assessment, notice of reassessment)
  • Notifications
  • Statements

If you made an enquiry through My Business in your CRA account, you may receive responses from the CRA for these electronic enquiries including:

  • Response to a formal dispute (notice of objection, appeal)
  • Response to an enquiry
  • Response to an audit enquiry submission

The CRA will use your business email address on file to send a notification when you have new mail in your CRA account. If you prefer to receive paper copies of your business correspondences, you can update your business mail preferences. Paper delivery will resume using the existing business mailing address the CRA has on file.

If you are registered for CRA email notifications and choose to receive paper mail, you will receive your business correspondence by paper mail, but may also receive email notifications.

Ways to change your business correspondence preferences

The fastest and easiest way to change how you receive your business correspondence is to sign to your CRA account to make this change online.


Online

Processing time: Immediate

You can manage how you receive your business correspondence in your CRA account. There is no wait time and it is updated immediately after you submit the changes.

Steps to change how you receive your business correspondence

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Business to access My Business Account
  3. Select Profile on the Overview page
  4. Select Manage mail for my business under the Manage Mail for my Business section
  5. Choose the delivery method you no longer want to receive (Online or Paper)
  6. Choose the program accounts you want to apply the change to
  7. Confirm your requested choices
  8. Confirm changes and select Submit

CRA sign in

For representatives with level 3 authorization

If you are an authorized representative, complete the following:

  1. Sign in to your CRA account
  2. Select Representative to access Represent a Client
  3. Enter the 9-digit Business number (BN) of the business you are representing and select Access BN
  4. Go to Step 3 for changing how you receive your business correspondence

To change a business address online for someone else, you must be an authorized representative who can access and update their business tax information.

If you are unable to access your CRA business information

To access your business information online, you must already have your BN connected to your CRA account.

For details: Access your business information online in your CRA account


By mail or fax

Processing time: Up to 10 business days after we receive your request

To change your business correspondence preferences by mail or fax, you must send a form to your tax centre.

  1. Complete Form RC681, Request to Activate Paper Mail for my business
  2. Sign the form to confirm you are requesting to receive paper mail for your business
  3. Send us your completed form within 6 months of the date it was signed to:

    • By mail

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. Mail your request to your tax centre

    • By fax

      1. Find your tax centre's mailing address using Find a CRA address
      2. To find the corresponding fax number for your tax centre, refer to Tax centres
      3. Send your request using the fax number for your tax centre

Make a change for someone else

To make this change for someone else, you must be an authorized representative with access to update their business tax information. The written request must clearly indicate you are making the change on behalf of the business and include your signature.
