Depending on your business structure and the changes you are making, there may be different actions you need to take.

On this page

  • Sole proprietorship

Sole proprietorship

A sole proprietorship is legally and directly tied to one specific individual. It cannot be changed or transferred to someone else.

If you sell or stop operating your business, you must close all of your CRA program accounts. Before closing your program accounts, you must report your business's final income on your personal income tax return and complete all other reporting obligations.

For details: Closing CRA program accounts

If someone else buys your business, they cannot use the same business number (BN). They will need to register for a new BN and program accounts for the business.

For details: Business registration with the CRA

Partnership

A partnership is considered to be a legal entity. When a new partner is added or an existing partner is removed, depending on the situation, the partnership may be considered as a new legal entity. If this is the case, the partnership may need to register for a new BN and program accounts.

In some cases, the partnership may be able to operate under the existing BN under certain situations:

  • The partner agreement states the business is allowed to use the same BN with a change in partners
  • There is no partnership agreement and an existing partner makes a request to continue operating under the existing BN
  • There is no change of partners, only a legal name change of one of the partners
